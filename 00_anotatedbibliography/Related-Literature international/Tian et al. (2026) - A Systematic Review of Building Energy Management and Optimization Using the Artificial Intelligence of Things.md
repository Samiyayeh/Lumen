# A Systematic Review of Building Energy Management and Optimization Using the Artificial Intelligence of Things (AIoT)

**Category:** [[Related Literature international]]

**Yunzhi Tian, Yuan Tian, Yi Jiang, and Vedran Mrzljak. 2026. A Systematic Review of Building Energy Management and Optimization Using the Artificial Intelligence of Things (AIoT). *Buildings* 16, 13 (2026), 2569. https://doi.org/10.3390/buildings16132569**

- **Summary:** Tian et al. conduct a PRISMA-compliant systematic literature review evaluating Artificial Intelligence of Things (AIoT) architectures in building energy management, examining high-frequency sensor telemetry pipelines, edge computing integration, and machine learning models for dynamic load optimization.

- **Relevance:** The review demonstrates that pairing real-time IoT telemetry with predictive intelligence overcomes the latency and visibility limitations of legacy building management systems, substantiating the end-to-end integration of edge microcontroller sensing with backend anomaly detection in the proposed system.

- **Source Reference(s):**
  - _Section 3 (AIoT Architectural Layers in Building Systems):_ Synthesizes multi-tier IoT architectures connecting edge telemetry collection with centralized cloud-based analytical engines.
  - _Section 4 (Algorithmic Paradigms and Energy Optimization):_ Reviews machine learning algorithms applied to high-frequency sensor streams to identify operational inefficiencies and reduce facility electricity costs.
