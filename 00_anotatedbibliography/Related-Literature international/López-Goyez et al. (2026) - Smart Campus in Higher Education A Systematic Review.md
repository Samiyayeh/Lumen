# Smart Campus in Higher Education: A Systematic Review of Intelligent Technologies, Information Systems, and Sustainable Digital Ecosystems

**Category:** [[Related Literature international]]

**Juan P. López-Goyez, Alfonso González-Briones, and Jhonatan Guaytarilla. 2026. Smart Campus in Higher Education: A Systematic Review of Intelligent Technologies, Information Systems, and Sustainable Digital Ecosystems. *Applied Sciences* 16, 14 (2026), 7277. https://doi.org/10.3390/app16147277**

- **Summary:** López-Goyez et al. conduct a systematic review of smart campus implementations worldwide, analyzing how IoT telemetry, AI analytics, and centralized information dashboards drive environmental sustainability and institutional electricity conservation.

- **Relevance:** The review demonstrates that deploying sensor-driven sub-metering infrastructure combined with automated data dashboards is critical for educational institutions seeking to eliminate campus energy waste, providing architectural validation for the proposed system.

- **Source Reference(s):**
  - _Section 3 (Intelligent Technologies in Campus Ecosystems):_ Evaluates the scalability and integration of IoT sensor networks, time-series telemetry pipelines, and centralized web interfaces in university facilities.
  - _Section 4 (Environmental Sustainability and Energy Efficiency):_ Synthesizes evidence showing significant electricity conservation achieved through automated load tracking and off-hours facility auditing.
