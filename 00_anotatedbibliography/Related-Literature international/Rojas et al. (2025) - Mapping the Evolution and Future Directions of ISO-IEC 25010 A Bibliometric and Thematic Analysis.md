# Mapping the Evolution and Future Directions of ISO/IEC 25010: A Bibliometric and Thematic Analysis

**Category:** [[Related Literature international]]

**Hesmeralda Rojas, Ronald Renteria, Virgilio Martinez Duran, Yuliana Tomaylla Gutiérrez, Manuel J. Ibarra Cabrera, and Afrig Aminuddin. 2025. Mapping the Evolution and Future Directions of ISO/IEC 25010: A Bibliometric and Thematic Analysis. *Engineering, Technology & Applied Science Research* 15, 5 (2025), 27530–27541. https://doi.org/10.48084/etasr.11772**

- **Summary:** Rojas et al. conduct a bibliometric and thematic analysis of 1,613 studies applying the ISO/IEC 25010 software product quality standard, evaluating the operationalization of characteristics including functional suitability, performance efficiency, reliability, security, and usability across applied software ecosystems.

- **Relevance:** The literature substantiates the standard's multidimensional evaluation framework as an internationally recognized benchmark for measuring software quality, establishing theoretical justification for using ISO/IEC 25010 quality metrics to evaluate user satisfaction and operational reliability in the proposed system.

- **Gap:** While the bibliometric analysis confirms ISO/IEC 25010 as the premier international benchmark for software product quality, the study highlights critical methodological gaps: the standard's rigid, hierarchical taxonomy struggles to accommodate modern agile development sprints and data-intensive AI pipelines. Furthermore, the literature notes a deficiency in standardized, operationalized evaluation instruments tailored for applied, multi-tier IoT architectures, making empirical validation of system reliability, latency, and usability challenging without customized hybrid metric frameworks.

- **Source Reference(s):**
  - _Section 3 (Bibliometric Trends and Thematic Clusters):_ Analyzes the widespread adoption of ISO/IEC 25010 quality characteristics across diverse information systems and connected platforms.
  - _Section 4 (Software Quality Evaluation Frameworks):_ Explores the standardized assessment methodologies used to evaluate system performance, security, and user-centric interaction.
