# Advances in Building Energy Management: A Comprehensive Review

**Category:** [[Related Literature international]]

**Muhammad Noman Shahid, Muhammad Usman Shahid, and Muhammad Irfan. 2025. Advances in Building Energy Management: A Comprehensive Review. *Buildings* 15, 23 (2025), 4237. https://doi.org/10.3390/buildings15234237**

- **Summary:** Shahid, Shahid, and Irfan conduct a comprehensive review of building energy management strategies, analyzing IoT telemetry integration, simulation modeling, and data interoperability challenges in mitigating structural facility energy waste.

- **Relevance:** The review demonstrates that implementing localized IoT monitoring architectures yields substantial reductions in institutional energy waste compared to static audit practices, providing technical justification for the real-time sub-metering dashboard developed for the proposed system.

- **Source Reference(s):**
  - _Section 2 (IoT-Based Building Energy Management Systems):_ Reviews empirical deployments demonstrating measurable energy reductions through automated, high-frequency IoT sensor telemetry.
  - _Section 5 (Implementation Challenges and Future Perspectives):_ Analyzes barriers in sensor interoperability, legacy panel integration, and the necessity for centralized monitoring dashboards.
