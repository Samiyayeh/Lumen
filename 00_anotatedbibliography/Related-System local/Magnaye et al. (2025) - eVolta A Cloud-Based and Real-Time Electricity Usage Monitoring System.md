# eVolta: A Cloud-Based and Real-Time Electricity Usage Monitoring System

**Category:** [[Related System local]]

**N. Magnaye, J. R. Saguid, R. M. Urate, and M. D. Malco. 2025. eVolta: A Cloud-Based and Real-Time Electricity Usage Monitoring System. *American Journal of Data Science and Artificial Intelligence* 1, 1 (2025), 1–10. DOI:https://doi.org/10.54536/ajdsai.v1i1.4884**

- **Summary:** Magnaye et al. develop eVolta, a cloud-based energy monitoring system tailored for Philippine electric consumers that pairs real-time hardware telemetry and utility notification integration with a secure, OTP-authenticated web dashboard to deliver live power tracking and threshold-based consumption alerts.

- **Gap:** The system relies strictly on static user-defined consumption thresholds and bulk consumer-level metering without sub-panel disaggregation, lacking machine learning-driven anomaly detection and context-aware academic or operational schedule correlation to identify off-hours idle loads.

- **Relevance:** The system's development under the Agile framework and empirical evaluation using the ISO/IEC 25010 quality standard confirm that localized real-time telemetry dashboards achieve high functional suitability and usability among facility users, providing methodological validation for the software quality evaluation plan and user interface design of the proposed system.

- **Source Reference(s):**
  - *Section 2 & Section 3 (System Architecture & Agile Implementation):* Technical design of the eVolta hardware-software infrastructure featuring OTP-based authentication, real-time consumption logging, and local utility cooperative integration.
  - *Section 4 (ISO/IEC 25010 Evaluation & Results):* Survey evaluation of 100 respondents across eight ISO/IEC 25010 quality characteristics, yielding an overall mean of 3.625 ("Very Effective") for functional suitability, efficiency, security, and usability.
