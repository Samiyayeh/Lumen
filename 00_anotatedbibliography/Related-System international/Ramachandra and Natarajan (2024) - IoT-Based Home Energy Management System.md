# State-of-the-art and real-time implementation of an IoT-based home energy management system for a cluster of dwellings

**Category:** [[Related System international]]

**Nikita Ramachandra and Rajasekar Natarajan. 2024. State-of-the-art and real-time implementation of an IoT-based home energy management system for a cluster of dwellings. *Heliyon* 10, 16 (2024), e35887. DOI:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.heliyon.2024.e35887**

- **Summary:** Ramachandra and Natarajan develop an IoT-driven energy management system that combines custom ESP32-based smart sub-metering hardware, multi-channel analog acquisition, and desktop and mobile monitoring applications to execute automated load profiling and peak power reduction.

- **Relevance:** The physical system validates that deploying multi-channel ESP32 edge metering hardware reduces peak load ratios by over fifty percent when tracking localized consumption, providing empirical justification for the edge microcontroller architecture and scheduled load auditing logic designed within the proposed system.

- **Source Reference(s):**
  - *Section 3.3 & Section 10.3 (Architecture of Smart Plug & Development of Smart Plug):* Physical hardware prototyping of a multi-channel metering plug utilizing the ESP32 microcontroller and dedicated power supply circuitry for localized load sensing.
  - *Section 10.1 & Section 10.2 (Mobile and Desktop Application Implementation):* Development of client monitoring interfaces providing real-time electrical telemetry display and appliance scheduling control.
