# Research on the Development of a Building Model Management System Integrating MQTT Sensing

**Category:** [[Related System international]]

**Ziang Wang, Han Xiao, Changsheng Guan, Liming Zhou, and Daiguang Fu. 2025. Research on the Development of a Building Model Management System Integrating MQTT Sensing. *Sensors* 25, 19 (2025), 6069. DOI:https://doi.org/10.3390/s25196069**

- **Summary:** Wang et al. develop an intelligent building management platform that bridges IoT sensor networks with interactive web-based browser interfaces using an MQTT-over-WebSocket protocol bridge to deliver sub-second data streaming and automated telemetry binding.

- **Relevance:** The system architecture demonstrates that routing MQTT sensor telemetry across WebSocket communication channels achieves browser data latency of 280 to 550 milliseconds, informing the network communication and web dashboard design of the proposed system to guarantee real-time electrical telemetry delivery for institutional facility administrators.

- **Source Reference(s):**
  - *Section 2 & Section 3 (System Architecture & MQTT-WebSocket Bridge):* Integration of the lightweight MQTT publish-subscribe messaging standard with full-duplex WebSocket connections to enable direct telemetry ingestion into web applications.
  - *Section 4 (Performance Evaluation and Latency Metrics):* Empirical testing demonstrating telemetry transmission latency between 280 ms and 550 ms alongside a 4.6/5 user satisfaction rating from facility managers.
