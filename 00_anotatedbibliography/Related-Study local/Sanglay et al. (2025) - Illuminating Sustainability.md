# Illuminating Sustainability: Innovative Approaches to Lighting Efficiency and Energy Conservation in Campus Environments

**Category:** [[Related Study local]]

**Marvin Ray G. Sanglay, Wilfredo B. Baniqued, and Orlando D. Davin Jr. 2025. Illuminating Sustainability: Innovative Approaches to Lighting Efficiency and Energy Conservation in Campus Environments. *Isabela State University Linker: Journal of Engineering, Computing and Technology* 2, 2 (2025). https://doi.org/10.65141/ject.v2i2.n6**

- **Summary:** Sanglay, Baniqued, and Davin evaluate electrical efficiency and illumination loads across academic facilities at Quirino State University, utilizing light measurement sensors and consumption analytics to identify baseline energy waste in campus classrooms and offices.

- **Gap:** The methodology is restricted to a manual, single-point cross-sectional audit of lighting fixtures using handheld lux meters, lacking continuous IoT edge telemetry, whole-circuit monitoring (such as HVAC and computing loads), and schedule-aware machine learning to autonomously isolate off-hours ghost consumption in real time.

- **Relevance:** The study confirms that room-level load profiling in Philippine universities identifies persistent lighting inefficiencies amounting to 1,252.14 kWh in monthly avoidable consumption, providing empirical justification for deploying localized sub-panel monitoring in the proposed system to isolate off-hours lighting and idle equipment waste.

- **Source Reference(s):**
  - *Section 1 (Introduction) & Section 2 (Methods, pp. 96–99):* Cross-sectional energy audit and single-point handheld lux measurement methodology across 67 rooms and 53 offices at Quirino State University Cabarroguis Campus.
  - *Section 3 (Results and Discussion, pp. 99–106):* Quantitative analysis demonstrating that artificial lighting in naturally daylit rooms accounts for 1,252.14 kWh per month in avoidable electrical draw.
  - *Section 4 (Conclusion, pp. 107–108):* Recommendations focusing on physical fixture retrofitting and manual behavioral adjustments, lacking automated real-time monitoring infrastructure.
