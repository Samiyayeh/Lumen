# Development of a Real-Time Electricity Meter Monitoring with Theft Detection Alert System

**Category:** [[Related Study local]]

**Jonilo C. Mababa. 2023. Development of a Real-Time Electricity Meter Monitoring with Theft Detection Alert System. *International Journal of Research and Innovation in Social Science* 7, 9 (2023), 886–893. DOI:https://doi.org/10.47772/IJRISS.2023.70976**

- **Summary:** Mababa develops an IoT-enabled electricity monitoring architecture to detect unauthorized power draw in real time, assessing software quality and system acceptability through an ISO/IEC 25010 evaluation framework.

- **Gap:** Anomaly identification is restricted to a simple differential comparison between two current sensors to catch physical wire tapping, lacking machine learning algorithms and institutional schedule cross-referencing to detect operational energy waste, idle appliance loads, or unscheduled off-hours ghost consumption.

- **Relevance:** The evaluation demonstrates that structured ISO/IEC 25010 criteria quantified via weighted mean calculations (yielding an overall score of 4.56 across functional suitability, security, and maintainability) effectively benchmark energy telemetry systems, providing direct methodological justification for the software evaluation framework used by the proposed system.

- **Source Reference(s):**
  - *Section II (Methodology, pp. 887–888):* System architecture integrating dual current sensors with an Arduino Mega and SIM800L GSM module for physical tap variance alerting.
  - *Section III & Section IV (System Evaluation & Results, pp. 889–893):* Quantitative assessment across 421 respondents using ISO/IEC 25010 criteria, achieving general weighted means across functional suitability (4.54), performance efficiency (4.57), and security (4.63).
