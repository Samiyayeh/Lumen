# B3Ø: Blynk-interfaced Three-Phase Smart Energy Monitoring System

**Category:** [[Related Study local]]

**I. K. I. Cubacub, S. N. C. Caburata, E. K. L. Calaguas, J. A. M. De Leon, M. P. Dimaano, I. F. R. Dizon, L. I. Dongallo, A. L. Tangcuangco, and R. L. D. Parungao. 2025. B3Ø: Blynk-interfaced Three-Phase Smart Energy Monitoring System. *International Journal of Electrical Engineering and Applied Sciences* 8, 2 (2025). DOI:https://doi.org/10.54554/ijeeas.2025.8.02.010**

- **Summary:** Cubacub et al. address the challenge of unmonitored load imbalances and power draw across Philippine university transformer banks by developing an IoT telemetry system that captures real-time electrical parameters across three-phase distribution circuits.

- **Gap:** The system is restricted to bulk transformer-bank distribution points and relies on the third-party Blynk mobile application for basic parameter visualization, lacking localized departmental sub-panel disaggregation, institutional database architecture, and schedule-aware machine learning to autonomously detect unscheduled off-hours ghost loads.

- **Relevance:** The empirical findings indicate that sub-metering institutional distribution panels achieves an energy measurement error margin of only 0.08% within ANSI C12.1-2008 standards, which validates the high-accuracy sensor telemetry layer utilized by the proposed system to monitor facility sub-circuits.

- **Source Reference(s):**
  - *Section 1 (Introduction) & Section 2 (System Design):* Hardware implementation and calibration of an IoT energy monitoring architecture deployed on a 230 V three-phase delta transformer bank at a state university in Pampanga.
  - *Section 3 (Experimental Results and Discussion):* Empirical validation of electrical parameter tracking demonstrating a 0.08% overall energy measurement error margin adhering to ANSI C12.1-2008 ±2% tolerance limits.
  - *Section 4 (Conclusion and Recommendation, p. 75):* Explicit acknowledgment that relying on Blynk constrains data monitoring capacity, recommending alternative microcontrollers and future analytical expansions.
