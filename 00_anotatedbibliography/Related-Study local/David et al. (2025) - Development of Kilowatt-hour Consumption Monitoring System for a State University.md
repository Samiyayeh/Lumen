# Development of Kilowatt-hour Consumption Monitoring System for a State University: Real-time Tracking with Sub-meter

**Category:** [[Related Study local]]

**J. D. David, A. T. Aga, J. C. Basa, T. M. Cailing Jr., A. E. Rimando VII, C. M. D. Canonero, and A. M. Viscayno. 2025. Development of Kilowatt-hour Consumption Monitoring System for a State University: Real-time Tracking with Sub-meter. *Asian Journal of Applied Science and Technology* 9, 3 (2025), 54–60. DOI:https://doi.org/10.38177/ajast.2025.9306**

- **Summary:** David et al. address the lack of granular electrical visibility across university departments by developing an IoT sub-metering infrastructure that records real-time kilowatt-hour data and visualizes localized consumption trends through a web dashboard.

- **Gap:** Although the system establishes department-level sub-metering, it remains a passive data logging tool that lacks automated anomaly detection and institutional schedule cross-referencing, requiring facility personnel to manually review historical bar graphs rather than receiving automated, real-time alerts for unscheduled off-hours ghost consumption.

- **Relevance:** The study confirms that sub-metering individual campus buildings disaggregates macro-utility bills and identifies department-level energy waste, providing empirical justification for deploying localized sub-panel telemetry within the proposed system to isolate building-level inefficiencies.

- **Source Reference(s):**
  - *Section 1 (Introduction) & Section 2 (System Development):* Implementation of an IoT kilowatt-hour tracking architecture deployed across state university buildings to replace conventional aggregated metering.
  - *Section 3 (Results and Discussion, pp. 56–59):* Evaluation of departmental load profiles, peak consumption intervals, and real-time dashboard data visualization.
  - *Section 4 (Conclusion, p. 58):* Explicit identification that the deployed platform lacks machine learning analytics, noting that predictive models are needed to optimize energy management.
