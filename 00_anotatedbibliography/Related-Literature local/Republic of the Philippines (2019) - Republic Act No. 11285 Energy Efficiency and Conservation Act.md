# Republic Act No. 11285: Energy Efficiency and Conservation Act and Its Implementing Guidelines for Institutional Facilities

**Category:** [[Related Literature local]]

**Republic of the Philippines. 2019. Republic Act No. 11285: An Act Institutionalizing Energy Efficiency and Conservation, Enhancing the Efficient Use of Energy, and Granting Incentives to Energy Efficiency and Conservation Projects. *Official Gazette of the Republic of the Philippines*. Retrieved from https://www.officialgazette.gov.ph/2019/04/12/republic-act-no-11285/**

- **Summary:** Republic Act No. 11285 establishes a national legal framework requiring commercial establishments and educational institutions to adopt formal energy management systems, periodic energy audits, and continuous electricity monitoring to systematically reduce institutional power waste.

- **Relevance:** The regulatory obligations and energy conservation targets specified under Republic Act No. 11285 provide the primary policy and compliance baseline for the proposed system, justifying the deployment of automated sub-metering and telemetry logs to replace inaccurate manual inspections.

- **Source Reference(s):**
  - _Section 4 (Objectives) & Section 16 (Energy Audits):_ Mandates that designated institutional establishments conduct systematic energy audits and submit standardized annual energy utilization and conservation reports.
  - _Section 18 (Government Energy Management Program):_ Directs government, state-supported, and academic entities to execute conservation programs aimed at reducing monthly electrical power consumption.
