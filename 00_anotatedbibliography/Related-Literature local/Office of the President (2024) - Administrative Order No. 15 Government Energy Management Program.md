# Administrative Order No. 15 (2024): Accelerating the Implementation of the Government Energy Management Program

**Category:** [[Related Literature local]]

**Office of the President of the Philippines. 2024. Administrative Order No. 15: Accelerating the Implementation of the Government Energy Management Program. *Official Gazette of the Republic of the Philippines*. Retrieved from https://www.officialgazette.gov.ph/2024/01/16/administrative-order-no-15-s-2024/**

- **Summary:** Administrative Order No. 15 directs government instrumentalities, public institutions, and educational facilities to accelerate the adoption of automated energy audits, real-time consumption monitoring, and energy conservation measures to reduce monthly institutional electricity usage.

- **Relevance:** The executive directives established under Administrative Order No. 15 provide immediate regulatory support for the proposed system, compelling institutional administrators to implement automated sub-metering and real-time usage telemetry to satisfy mandatory government energy reduction targets.

- **Source Reference(s):**
  - _Section 1 (Accelerated Implementation of GEMP):_ Mandates all public and state-funded institutional entities to enforce structured energy efficiency protocols and participate in comprehensive energy management programs.
  - _Section 3 (Monitoring and Compliance):_ Requires regular digital tracking of facility electrical consumption and systematic reporting of energy audit data to the Inter-Agency Energy Efficiency and Conservation Committee.
