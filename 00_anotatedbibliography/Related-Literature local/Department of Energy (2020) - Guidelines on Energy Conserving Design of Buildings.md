# Department of Energy Circular No. DC2020-12-0026: Guidelines on Energy Conserving Design and Sub-Metering for Commercial and Institutional Buildings

**Category:** [[Related Literature local]]

**Department of Energy. 2020. Department Circular No. DC2020-12-0026: Adoption of the Guidelines on Energy Conserving Design of Buildings. *Republic of the Philippines Department of Energy*. Retrieved from https://www.doe.gov.ph/laws-and-issuances/department-circular-no-dc2020-12-0026**

- **Summary:** Department Circular No. DC2020-12-0026 establishes mandatory national energy efficiency standards that require commercial and educational facilities to implement dedicated electrical sub-metering systems and energy management controls across distinct operational zones.

- **Relevance:** The technical directives defined under Department Circular No. DC2020-12-0026 substantiate the sub-metering architecture of the proposed system, validating the division of campus infrastructure into discrete monitored zones rather than relying solely on macro-level utility meters.

- **Source Reference(s):**
  - _Section 6 (Electrical Power and Lighting Systems):_ Prescribes the installation of independent sub-metering equipment for major electrical distribution sub-panels in institutional facilities.
  - _Section 8 (Building Energy Management):_ Mandates automated data recording and power monitoring protocols to track consumption baselines and identify anomalous electrical usage.
